I am 朱虹翱 (pinyin: ZHŪ HÓNG ÁO), a linguistics senior from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), Shanghai, China. 

### Research
I have been engaging with computational linguistics, and long to pursue a Ph.D. program in this field. I am deeply intrigued by research on the interpretations of LLM's linguistic capabilities and the learnability of language.

My research focus is computational linguistics, specifically on cognitive modeling, multilingual syntactic and pragmatic benchmarking, authorship attribution for AI detection, as well as critical discourse analysis of news media. I have been collaborating with Professor <a href="https://www.asc.ohio-state.edu/schuler.77" target="_blank">William Schuler</a> at Ohio State University beginning the summer of 2024, investigating the psychometric predictive power of small LMs and their alignment with human cognitive behaviors. Meanwhile, I partake in the computational linguistics lab at SJTU as a research assistant, training Transformer LMs to the interpretation of child language acquisition.

In my own research, I manipulated the training data of LMs to capture the machine's acquisition trajectory of grammar (then tested it with metrics other than minimal pairs), and I also devised an interpretable AI detection system combining stylistics metrics and machine learning.
