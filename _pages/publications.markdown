### 2026

**Hongao Zhu**, Muxiaoqiao Xu, Yikang Liu, Siyuan Song, Yuxia Wang, Byung-Doh Oh, Hai Hu. (2026). A Systematic Analysis of the Predictive Power of LM Surprisal in Reading Chinese. *ArXiv preprint arXiv:2610.04898*. [[pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.04898)]<br>
*Surprisal predicts Chinese reading times, but whether bigger models predict better depends on the eye-tracking corpus.*

Yi-Chien Lin, **Hongao Zhu**, William Schuler. (2026). Vectors from Larger Language Models Predict Human Reading Time and fMRI Data More Poorly when Dimensionality Expansion is Controlled. To appear in *EMNLP 2026*. [[pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.12196)] [[code](https://github.com/nicalin/llm-vec-dim-control)]<br>
*Controlling for dimensionality, larger models predict reading times and fMRI worse, and the benefit of training vanishes at a few billion parameters on most datasets.*

Yikang Liu, Yeting Shen, **Hongao Zhu**, Lilong Xu, Zhiheng Qian, Siyuan Song, Kejia Zhang, Jialong Tang, Pei Zhang, Baosong Yang, Rui Wang, Hai Hu. (2026). A Systematic Assessment of Language Models with Linguistic Minimal Pairs in Chinese. *Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics, 14*, 755–771. [[ACL anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.tacl-1.34/)] [[pdf](https://aclanthology.org/2026.tacl-1.34.pdf)] [[data](https://github.com/sjtu-compling/ZhoBLiMP)] [[models](https://huggingface.co/collections/SJTU-CL/zh-pythia-6734b40c21823ee4ea28de8f)]<br>
*118 paradigms across 15 phenomena; anaphora, quantifiers, and ellipsis stay hard even for 32B-parameter models.*

Siyuan Song, Zhiheng Qian, Yunhao Zhang, Linyang He, Xiaozhe Ji, Yingxin Lin, **Hongao Zhu**, Chongtian Shao, Chuhan Lang, Luan Li, Rui Wang, Renfen Hu, Shaonan Wang, Hai Hu. (2026). The First ChineseBabyLM Challenge: Training Data-Efficient and Cognitively Plausible Language Models for Chinese. *ArXiv preprint arXiv:2607.10745*. [[pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.10745)] [[data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/chinese-babylm-org/babylm-zho-100M)] [[evaluation](https://github.com/SiyuanSong2004/chinese-babylm-eval-pipeline)]

Qingwen Zhao\*, **Hongao Zhu**\*, Yunqi He, Rui Wang, Aijun Huang, Hai Hu. (2026). LLMs for Automatic Annotation of Mandarin Narrative Transcripts. *ArXiv preprint arXiv:2605.17205*. [[pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.17205)] (\*equal contribution)<br>
*The best LLM agreed with human annotators at κ = .794 (human–human: .872) and cut annotation time by 65%.*
