Along with graduation, Hong'ao was awarded Wenzhi New Scholar Award (文治新人) for 2025.

Hong'ao was the only undergraduate student awarded for 2025, and he was also the first to be nominated in the history of the [School of Foreign Languages](https://sfl.sjtu.edu.cn/) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU).

[The Wenzhi New Scholar Award](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/tg/20250530/211162.html) is a prestigious academic honor. It is designed to guide and motivate students in the fields of humanities and social sciences, as well as interdisciplinary areas combining humanities with science, engineering, agriculture, and medicine, to cultivate academic aspirations, enhance research capabilities, and adhere to academic ethics.

This highly selective award is presented annually to no more than 10 recipients, including both graduate and undergraduate nominees. It aims to recognize students who have demonstrated outstanding academic quality and achieved significant innovative academic results during their studies at SJTU.
